Featured project / Case study

Resident Benefits
Management System

A full-stack platform I built from the ground up to support resident management, benefits tracking, reporting, user access, auditability and QR workflows.

AngularTypeScriptNode.jsExpressMongoDBGCP
01 / Overview

From requirements
to deployed product.

I owned the solution end-to-end: requirements, architecture, frontend, backend APIs, database, authentication, role-based access, testing, troubleshooting and Google Cloud deployment.

The system was designed around a large resident dataset and multiple staff roles, with controlled access to resident information and administrative workflows.
